# Luxury Reel direction — 2026-10-05

## What was reviewed

- Louis Vuitton's public Instagram profile and Reel grid. Direct Reel playback
  required an Instagram login, so no private or login-gated material was used.
- Louis Vuitton's public Spring–Summer 2026 campaign page, for its use of
  movement, close detail, human presence, and restrained brand framing.
- Meta's public Reels guidance and recommendation updates.

## Direction for ZARI

The useful pattern is a short visual world rather than a product lesson: begin
with a material or human detail, change scale through several confident cuts,
let music determine the edit, and reserve typography for the final brand beat.
The reference informs rhythm and restraint; it does not supply footage, copy,
layouts, logos, or protected campaign elements.

For this test, the first 1.5 seconds use three half-beat cuts. The middle expands
to one-second shots, and the final 1.5 seconds hold long enough for the brand
reveal. The carpet remains present in every shot. This changes the opening and
editing premise while keeping the format, length, palette, and posting intent
fixed for later comparison.
