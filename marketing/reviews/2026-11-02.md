## Learning from the last week

The verified export for 2026-10-26 through 2026-11-01 is unavailable. The
2026-11-01 row in `marketing/metrics.csv` therefore marks reach, views,
3-second hold, average watch time, completion, replays, likes, interactions,
engaged accounts, saves, shares, comments, profile visits, follower change, and
qualified conversations unavailable. The strongest and weakest post for that
week cannot be ranked from verified evidence.

The latest earlier verified account snapshot (week ending 2026-10-04) showed
0 views, 1 interaction, and 1 engaged account across three posts. `01-meet-zari`
had the only interaction; `02-from-bhadohi` and `03-follow-the-making` tied at
zero interactions. Reach, saves, shares, and follower movement were unavailable.
This is a weak directional baseline, not evidence of a winning format.

**Repeat:** carpet-first framing, tangible craft, deliberate camera movement,
and restrained luxury styling. The prior visual sequence scored 4/5, and the
approved direction asks for cuts that answer the music.

**Stop:** the bouncing push toward the carpet, static-image edits that only
animate text, utility-led rug-care topics, and generic sales language. Those
directions conflict with the exact feedback: “remove this bouncy movement to
carpet”; “it hardly feels like a video, it feels like just picture with text in
and out”; “a luxury brand should not post how to fold a rug”; and “this feels
too much like ad and not like engaging content.”

**Improve:** make the rug or craft detail visible in the opening second, use
actual movement, and keep text minimal. The three Reels hold the proposed 9-second
runtime, native audio excerpt, restrained typography, caption question, and
19:30 IST window constant while varying edit cadence: cuts every two beats,
cuts every four beats, and one continuous camera glide. This is a directional
movement/edit-rhythm comparison; the source scenes differ, so it is not a
controlled causal A/B test. Compare verified 3-second hold and average watch
time first, with completion, replays, saves, profile visits, and qualified
conversations as supporting signals.
