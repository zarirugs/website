# Instagram Reel review rubric

Apply this gate to every Reel before it is presented for approval. Review the
finished video with sound on and again without sound.

## Immediate rejection

Replace or recut a Reel without scoring it if it uses unlicensed music, presents
stock media as a ZARI product, begins as a static slideshow, contains a generic
sales CTA, or exports below 1080 × 1920. The user's rejection always overrides a
numeric score.

## Score each surviving Reel

Score each category from 1 to 5. A Reel must reach 21/25, score at least 4 in
aesthetic strength and authenticity, and pass the technical checks.

| Category | Review question |
| --- | --- |
| Aesthetic strength | Would the opening and edit stop the right person in a premium fashion or interiors feed? |
| Brand relevance | Is the carpet the visual subject, with a restrained and recognisable ZARI point of view? |
| Authenticity | Does the footage feel real, specific, and free of synthetic or misleading product claims? |
| Pacing and sound | Do movement, cuts, and music form one deliberate rhythm? |
| Technical execution | Is the 9:16 export crisp, safe-zone aware, correctly mixed, and free of visible artifacts? |

Also score the complete weekly sequence from 1 to 5 for rhythm and variety. It
must score 4 or 5 before presentation.

Prefer carpet-led movement, confident crops, editorial restraint, and cuts that
answer the music. A catchphrase is optional. Avoid instructional framing unless
the approved concept is explicitly educational.
