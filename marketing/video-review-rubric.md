# Instagram Reel review rubric

Use this gate for every Reel before it enters the weekly approval pack. A Reel must score at least 21/25, with hook strength, authenticity and technical execution each at least 4/5.

## Immediate rejection

Reject a Reel without scoring when it contains synthetic motion or distorted objects, misrepresents another maker's work as ZARI, uses an unclear rug subject, repeats previously published footage without a deliberate reason, includes unlicensed media, or fails Instagram's 9:16 playback requirements.

## Score from 1 to 5

| Category | Review question |
| --- | --- |
| Hook strength | Is the rug or useful idea clear within the first second? |
| Brand relevance | Does it build ZARI's world of rugs, material, craft and considered interiors? |
| Authenticity | Does the source feel real and avoid synthetic motion or false product claims? |
| Pacing and story | Does each beat earn its place and resolve within 6–15 seconds? |
| Technical execution | Is it crisp 1080×1920 H.264, legible in safe areas, smoothly compressed and playable without sound? |

Review the Reel both muted and with audio. Text must carry the idea when muted. Reject any shaky or stepped synthetic camera movement. Prefer locked framing unless real footage supports natural motion.

Music must use one of two recorded routes:

- **Automated:** a commercially cleared track from Meta Sound Collection or another documented royalty-free license is mixed into the MP4 before publishing.
- **Trending:** an English, Punjabi or instrumental track is selected from the music library available inside the ZARI Instagram account. Record the exact track and approved excerpt; never download and embed a commercial song from another source.

Professional business accounts may have a smaller licensed-music catalog, and availability varies by region. Every weekly review must therefore show the track, language, excerpt and rights route, or explicitly say `No music selected`.
