## Creative decision

Return to the first visual language: one strong carpet image, restrained forward movement and concise editorial text. The A/B/C craft-footage concepts are rejected. Do not force the `RUG CHECK.` signature into this direction.

Future tests should preserve this visual structure and change one item at a time: opening line, movement speed, or music. Judge the result by 3-second hold rate, average watch time, completion rate, saves and profile visits.
