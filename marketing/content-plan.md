**Name:** ZARI | Hand-knotted rugs

**Bio:**

> Rugs rooted in Indian craftsmanship.<br>
> Follow the making of ZARI.<br>

Pin the first three posts so a new visitor immediately understands the brand,
origin, and reason to follow.

## Distribution target

- First 25: founders' personal networks, past professional contacts, and WhatsApp status.
- 25–60: consistent useful posts plus daily participation in relevant interior and craft conversations.
- 60–85: one genuine collaboration with a small interior, architecture, or craft account.
- 85–100: repeat the most saved/shared topic and ask existing followers to share it with one design-minded person.

Do not buy followers, automate comments, mass-DM strangers, or run a broad giveaway.
Those tactics create the wrong audience and can damage recommendation eligibility.
